About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate at The University of Queensland (UQ), supervised by A/Prof. Xin Yu. I am currently a visiting Ph.D. student at MMLab@NTU, advised by Prof. Chen Change Loy and Dr. Shangchen Zhou. I also work as a Ph.D. student researcher at CSIRO's Data61, collaborating with Dr. Dadong Wang. My research interests lie in computer vision and generative models, with a particular focus on efficient low-level vision, compressed video processing, and world foundation models.
